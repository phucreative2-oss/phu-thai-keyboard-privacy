PAANPIMP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PAANPIMP, developed by Piyapong Thongon, handles information when you use the app on HarmonyOS.

1. Keyboard Input

PAANPIMP provides Thai and English keyboard input. Text entered using the keyboard is processed locally on your device to provide typing functionality. The current version does not transmit typed text or passwords to the developer's servers.

2. Information Collection and Local Storage

The current version does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information for storage on the developer's servers. Keyboard preferences and app settings may be stored locally on your device to remember your choices.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

The current version does not sell or share typed text, passwords, or locally stored keyboard preferences with third parties. If future versions introduce features that involve additional data processing or third-party services, this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Data Retention and Security

Keyboard preferences and settings may remain on your device until you change them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, depending on device behavior. The developer does not operate a server that stores your typed text or passwords in the current version.

5. Children's Privacy

PAANPIMP is a general-purpose keyboard app and is not specifically directed at children. The current version does not knowingly collect children's personal information for storage on the developer's servers. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of their devices as appropriate.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage the app's settings and permissions using the app and your device's system settings. You may clear locally stored app data or uninstall the app using your device's available controls. For questions about privacy or applicable data protection rights, please contact the developer.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality or data handling changes. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
